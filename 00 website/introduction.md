# NANAL — English Website Copy (Reference)

## Tagline
- Main: **Turning your special day into a memory to keep.**
- Alternatives: "Every special day deserves a gift." / "A gift for the one who matters most."

## One-line Introduction
NANAL is a gift-like brand that creates everything for the star of a special day, from custom cards and space styling to party supplies.

## About NANAL

### Our Name
"Nanal" (나날) is a native Korean word meaning "day after day, every day."

### Our Vision
Life can be hard and tiring, but the happiest, most precious days stay with us for a long time. At NANAL, we turn those days into memories you can keep for years to come.

### What We Do
NANAL designs goods and styles spaces for the once-in-a-lifetime days that matter most.

- **Custom Cards**: Cards made for the person you're celebrating.
- **Event Space Styling**: We develop a concept for your event space and bring it to life.
- **Party Supplies & Props**: Balloons, party goods, and props, all made by NANAL.

## How We Work
- **Made Just for You**: Each piece is shaped by the story and taste of the person it's for.
- **NANAL Originals**: Products we plan, design, and make ourselves, available in our shop.
